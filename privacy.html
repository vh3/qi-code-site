Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 September 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how MorsePath (“the App”), published by Brain Fart Games, handles information. The App is a local Morse code trainer. Your practice data stays on your device. Optional Extended Content purchases are processed by Apple, not by Brain Fart Games.

Who we are

Contact: bfgames.support@gmail.com

What the App stores on your device

  • Your settings (speed, sending input mode, letter display, and the sound, visual, and haptic toggles)
  • Which characters you are learning, and per-character practice evidence such as accuracy and response times
  • A record of individual practice attempts, used to decide when a new character is introduced
  • A short history of recent session summaries

This is written to a single file in the App’s own storage on your device. The App does not create an account and does not sync anything to Brain Fart Games servers. If you turn on “Practice without saving,” that session is not written to the file at all.

What we do not collect

  • No accounts, names, email addresses, or contact details
  • No advertising identifiers, and no advertising of any kind
  • No third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs
  • No location, microphone, camera, contacts, or photo access — the App requests no permissions
  • No sale or sharing of personal information

Guided practice and Full Morse Practice work without a network connection. Purchasing or restoring Extended Content uses Apple’s StoreKit and requires a network connection to Apple’s servers.

In-app purchases

  • The App may offer a one-time Extended Content unlock through Apple’s In-App Purchase system.
  • Payment processing, receipt validation, and related purchase records are handled by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy. Brain Fart Games does not receive your payment card details.
  • The App stores only a local flag that Extended Content is unlocked on this device (via StoreKit entitlements), so it can continue the Koch course past the free set.
  • Full Morse Practice remains available without purchase.

Sharing a practice record

After a session you can tap “Share practice record” to send a short plain-text summary using the standard iOS share sheet. This happens only when you choose it, and the text goes wherever you send it. Brain Fart Games does not receive a copy.

Deleting your data

“Reset guided progress” in Settings clears your practice evidence on the device. Deleting the App removes its stored file entirely.

Diagnostics and crashes

When you install builds through TestFlight or the App Store, Apple may collect crash and usage diagnostics according to your device settings and Apple’s policies. Brain Fart Games may review crash reports Apple makes available in App Store Connect or Xcode Organizer. See Apple’s privacy information for those platforms.

Children

The App does not target children for data collection. It does not ask for names, emails, or account details anywhere, and it contains no advertising. Optional Extended Content is an App Store purchase processed by Apple.

Changes

If this policy changes in a material way — for example if a future release adds accounts, cloud sync, advertising, or remote analytics — we will update this page and the “Last updated” date.

Contact

Privacy questions: bfgames.support@gmail.com
