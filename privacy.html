Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 August 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Qi Code (“the App”), published by BFG, handles information. The App is a local, offline Morse code trainer. Your practice data stays on your device.

Who we are

Contact: bfgames.support@gmail.com

What the App stores on your device

  • Your settings (speed, sending input mode, letter display, and the sound, visual, and haptic toggles)
  • Which characters you are learning, and per-character practice evidence such as accuracy and response times
  • A record of individual practice attempts, used to decide when a new character is introduced
  • A short history of recent session summaries

This is written to a single file in the App’s own storage on your device. The App does not create an account and does not sync anything to BFG servers. If you turn on “Practice without saving,” that session is not written to the file at all.

What we do not collect

  • No accounts, names, email addresses, or contact details
  • No advertising identifiers, and no advertising of any kind
  • No third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs
  • No location, microphone, camera, contacts, or photo access — the App requests no permissions
  • No sale or sharing of personal information
  • No network use at all — the App works in Airplane Mode immediately after install

Sharing a practice record

After a session you can tap “Share practice record” to send a short plain-text summary using the standard iOS share sheet. This happens only when you choose it, and the text goes wherever you send it. BFG does not receive a copy.

Deleting your data

“Reset guided progress” in Settings clears your practice evidence on the device. Deleting the App removes its stored file entirely.

Diagnostics and crashes

When you install builds through TestFlight or the App Store, Apple may collect crash and usage diagnostics according to your device settings and Apple’s policies. BFG may review crash reports Apple makes available in App Store Connect or Xcode Organizer. See Apple’s privacy information for those platforms.

Children

The App does not target children for data collection. It does not ask for names, emails, or account details anywhere, and it contains no advertising and no in-app purchases.

Changes

If this policy changes in a material way — for example if a future release adds accounts, cloud sync, advertising, or remote analytics — we will update this page and the “Last updated” date.

Contact

Privacy questions: bfgames.support@gmail.com
