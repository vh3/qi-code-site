Common questions

I can’t hear anything

Qi Code is an audio-first trainer. Turn the volume up, and check that “Audio” is on in Settings. Every sound is also shown as a visual pulse, and “Haptics” adds vibration, so the exercises still work with sound off.

Why does it play so fast at the start?

Each character is always sent at full character speed, with extra silence between characters while you are still slow. Learning a stretched-out letter teaches a sound you would later have to unlearn. Both speeds are adjustable in Settings: “Character speed” is how fast a letter itself is keyed, and “Effective speed” controls the space between letters.

Why won’t it give me new characters?

New characters unlock once your accuracy on the current set supports it. The recap screen after each session explains what it is waiting for. You can also turn off “Add new characters automatically” in Settings.

Do I have to press and hold to send?

No. By default, Dot and Dash are two separate large buttons and your timing is not judged. A press-and-hold paddle is available in Settings for anyone who wants it, but it is never required.

Is the practice record a merit badge sign-off?

No. Qi Code is a generic Morse trainer and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or licensed by any Scouting organization. The shared record says in plain text that it is practice evidence only. Always check the current official requirements with a counselor.
